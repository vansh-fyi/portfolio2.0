# Robotics Research: VLAs, LLMs and the SO-ARM101

## Overview
Alongside his work at Tapforce, Vansh is an independent robotics researcher. His research looks at the relationships between **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models**, **large language models (LLMs)** and **robots**, and at how to optimise them, working toward **physics-aware general intelligence**. He hopes to pursue a PhD soon.

The work is ongoing and is intended for publication at a major conference, so only a brief overview is shared here. More will be available once it is published.

## The Robot
Vansh has built his research setup around the **SO-ARM101**, a low-cost open-source robot arm.

## The Models
The models involved include:
- **OpenVLA-OFT**
- **π0 and π0.5**
- **SmolVLA**
- **ACT**
- **Gemini Robotics**
- Multimodal LLMs such as **Claude**

## Status
Underway since July 2026. Methods and results will be shared after publication.
