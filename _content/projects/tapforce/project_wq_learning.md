# Case Study: WorldQuant Learning (Keystone Website)

## Key Achievements & Contributions
- **First Version of the Site**: In August 2026 Vansh built the first version of the Keystone marketing website for WorldQuant Learning as a static site.
- **Home Hero Design**: In September 2026 he contributed the design of the full-bleed home hero stage to the later Next.js version of the site, which other developers on the team also worked on.
- **A Shared Project**: This is occasional work alongside Vansh's main Tapforce projects, and he is one contributor among several.

## 1. Context: What Keystone Is
Keystone is described on the site as an intelligent curriculum platform from WorldQuant Learning. The marketing site explains the platform and speaks to three audiences: higher education, corporate learning, and continuing professional education.

## 2. What Vansh Built
- **The first site**: A multi-page site with a home page, an About page with the leadership team, audience pages under "Who we serve", resources, blog and news pages, and accessibility, privacy, cookie and terms pages.
- **Navigation and visual system**: A sub-navigation with feature dropdowns and consistent labeled cards across blog, resources and news.
- **The home hero**: A full-bleed "stage" hero that opens the first chapter of the home page in the Next.js version.

## 3. Status
Ongoing, occasional work. The Next.js site is a team project and Vansh's part is the first static version and the home hero design.
