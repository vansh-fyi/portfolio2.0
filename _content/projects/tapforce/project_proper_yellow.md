# Case Study: Proper Yellow

## Key Achievements & Contributions
- **A Video-First Experience**: The site is built around the studio's films, with a hero, a portfolio of work and a clear process.
- **Works Across Devices**: A vertical, full-screen video feed on mobile and a dedicated mode for landscape viewing, with the correct video staying in place when a phone is rotated.
- **Fast Video Delivery**: Video is served through Cloudflare Stream for quick loading.
- **From Viewing to Booking**: Calls-to-action open an online booking flow, and Google Analytics tracks visits.

## 1. Context: Who Proper Yellow Is
Proper Yellow is a video production company that makes commercials, brand stories and interviews. Its positioning is "We make your stories pop" and "Cinematic storytelling, not just ads." Services run from concept development through production and post-production (editing, color grading and sound design) to final delivery.

## 2. What Vansh Built
- **Hero**: A video hero with time markers (10s to 50s) that frames the studio's range of work.
- **Work section**: A portfolio of the studio's films and ads, such as the short film *The Little Match Girl*, the series pilot *The House*, and the *EDDA* advertising series.
- **Process section**: A simple three-step explanation of how the studio works.
- **Contact and booking**: Contact information and booking calls-to-action.

## 3. Engineering Decisions
- **No framework needed**: The site is plain HTML, JavaScript and Tailwind CSS, which keeps it light and fast for a media-heavy page.
- **Careful video handling**: Much of the work went into smooth mobile playback, including the feed's scroll behavior and keeping the right video playing after an iPhone rotates.
- **Automated tests**: Playwright end-to-end tests are set up for the site.

## 4. Status
Live at properyellow.com. Active development ran from December 2025 to May 2026.
