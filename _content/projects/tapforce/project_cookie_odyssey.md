# Case Study: The Cookie Odyssey Mobile App

## Key Achievements & Contributions
- **Design Before Code**: The project began with a product brief, a trigger map, UX scenarios, a design system and a product requirements document before the app was built.
- **A Complete Ordering Flow**: Browsing, choosing cookies, checkout, payment, and order tracking for both delivery and pickup.
- **Brand-True Interface**: The app is meant to feel like a digital window into the shop, not a high-tech product.

## 1. Context: The Brand
The Cookie Odyssey is a New York City bakery established in 1978 in Midtown Manhattan, known for its Triple Belgian Chocolate Chunk cookie. The vision for the app is a digital version of the physical shop: warm, wholesome and hand-packed. The main goal is frictionless order-to-door delivery and pickup.

## 2. Design Direction
- **Palette**: Azure blue, taken from the logo script, on soft whites and grays.
- **Tone**: Traditional, welcoming and artisanal. The design avoids sci-fi or futuristic effects.
- **Motion**: Subtle, organic micro-animations, such as cookies settling into the box.
- **Product focus**: Cookie packs (2, 3 and 5), mini cookies, and drink pairings.

## 3. What Vansh Is Building
- **Screens**: Splash and home, the full menu, a cookie detail sheet, checkout, payment success, order tracking, and a profile tab.
- **Ordering modes**: Delivery and pickup both flow from checkout through to tracking.
- **Payments and data**: Stripe for payments, and Supabase for the backend, including real-time order status in the tracking screen.
- **Quality checks**: Structured user-acceptance testing and code reviews in each phase.

## 4. Status
In progress. Design and the core ordering screens have been built and tested, and development is continuing.
